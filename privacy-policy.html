FlowPath Privacy Policy

FlowPath is a block-sliding puzzle game for Android and iOS.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play FlowPath. The short version: we run no servers and we keep no account for you. Your progress stays on your phone. What reaches the outside world is advertising (served by Google), anonymous gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and, if you buy something, the record of that purchase (RevenueCat).

What stays on your phone

Everything the game remembers about you is stored in the app's own storage on your device: levels finished, stars, coins, gems, boosters, which worlds are unlocked, your daily reward streak, and your settings (music, sound, vibration, left- or right-handed play).

We cannot read any of it. It is not sent anywhere, it is not backed up to us, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

FlowPath shows ads through Google AdMob. Google, not us, collects and processes the data behind those ads. According to Google, its Mobile Ads SDK collects:

What Why
IP address To serve ads and estimate roughly where the device is
Advertising identifier and app set ID To limit repeats, measure ads and — with consent — personalise them
Ad interactions (views, taps, video views, app opens) To measure and bill ads, and to detect fraud
Diagnostics (crashes, performance) To keep the ad SDK working

How Google uses this is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can see the companies Google may share ad data with at Google's list of ad technology providers.

Gameplay statistics

To see which levels are too hard and which parts of the game people use, FlowPath sends anonymous events to Google Analytics for Firebase: a level was started, won, lost or left (with its number, stars and moves), a booster or a continue was used, an ad was watched, a screen was opened. Along with them go a random app instance ID, the device model and system version, and a rough location worked out by Google from the IP address. No name, email or anything you type is sent.

Where the consent form below applies, these statistics follow your answer to it. How Google handles this data is described in Google Analytics data collection.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, FlowPath may remind you — at most once a day, in the evening — that a Free Chest or daily reward is waiting. These reminders are planned on your phone by the game itself. No server sends them, and nothing about you is collected to do it. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings for FlowPath.

Your choices about ads

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, FlowPath asks for your consent the first time you open it, before any ad is requested. If you say no to personalised ads, you still get ads — just ones that are not based on a profile of you.
  • Where that consent applies, you can change your mind at any time: open Shop → Privacy → Ad privacy options in the game.
  • On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your system settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Buying Remove Ads in the shop stops the ads between levels and the banner on the home screen. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available, because you ask for those yourself.

Purchases

If you buy anything in FlowPath, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card number, your address or your name — only whether a purchase went through, so the game can give you what you bought.

To check that a purchase is genuine and to let you restore it on a new phone, the store's receipt is verified by RevenueCat, a service that works for us. It receives the purchase record (what was bought, when, the order ID) and a random ID for this install — nothing that names you.

Children

FlowPath is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from a child. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for teen and general audiences.

If you believe a child has been using the app and you would like their device data cleared, uninstalling the app removes everything the game stored. You can also write to us at the address below.

What we never do

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name or email.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, files, precise location or your microphone.
  • We do not sell your data, and nothing we receive can tell us who you are.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete personal data held about you, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold nothing ourselves, the fastest routes are the ad choices listed above, and uninstalling the app. For anything held by Google as a result of the ads, use your Google Account controls. If you would like help with a request, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
